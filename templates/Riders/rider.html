Rider requirements

What you'll need to get started

Simple basics — if you have these, you're ready to apply.

Valid Government ID

A national ID, passport, or driver's license for identity verification.

Driver's License

A valid driver's license if you plan to deliver by car or motorcycle.

A Vehicle

Motorcycle, bicycle, scooter, or car — whatever works best for your area.

Smartphone

An internet-connected smartphone to receive orders and navigate.

Bank Account

A valid bank account in your name to receive your weekly or daily payouts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before hitting the road.

Click the "Become a Rider" button, complete the 6-step application form, and submit your documents. Our team reviews your application and approves most riders within 24–48 hours.
Your earnings are deposited directly to the bank account you provided during registration. Standard payouts run weekly, and eligible riders can cash out daily for a small fee.
You'll need a valid government-issued ID, your driver's license (if delivering by motorcycle or car), and your bank account details. Vehicle registration and proof of address are optional.
